01 / The hotel

A place people come for the sea and stay for the atmosphere.

CASA BRAVA is a small hotel with around 20 rooms, just a short walk from the sea.

It’s easy to spend a few days here at your own pace: a slow breakfast in the morning, a trip to the beach or along the coast, back to the pool after a walk, and a drink on the terrace that lingers into the evening.

Everything you need for a short break on the Costa Brava — the sea, the sun, and time you don’t have to plan in advance.

06 / A few days at CASA BRAVA

What your day here could look like.

  1. I

    Morning

    Wake up without an alarm. Come down for breakfast, have a coffee and decide where to go today.

  2. II

    Day

    The sea, beaches and small coves of the Costa Brava. A walk along the coast or a trip to one of the places nearby.

  3. III

    After the beach

    Come back to CASA BRAVA and spend a couple of hours by the pool. There’s nowhere else you need to be.

  4. IV

    Evening

    Head out to dinner at one of the restaurants nearby, then return to the hotel and end the day with a drink on the terrace.

And in the morning, start again — perhaps in a completely different way.
